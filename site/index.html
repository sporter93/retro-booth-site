FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Do we own the booth?

Yes. Retro Booth sells photo booths for you to own, either outright or with finance, subject to eligibility.

Are there ongoing costs?

Yes. Every booth runs with an ongoing software and support subscription. We'll walk you through what's included when we prepare your quote.

How much does a booth cost?

It depends on the model, finish and customisation you choose. Tell us about your space and we'll prepare a tailored quote.

Can the booth be customised?

Yes. Exterior finishes, venue branding, photo strip designs and the digital experience can all be tailored, subject to availability.

Do our staff need to run it?

No. Guests use the booth themselves, so your team can stay focused on service.

What about guest data?

Data capture is optional. If you use it for marketing, guests must give their consent.

Can I start a photo booth business with Retro Booth?

Yes. We work with entrepreneurs as well as venues. Let's talk about your plans.
