FAQ

Questions venues ask.

Can we include the booth in our packages?

Yes. For a package event the booth switches into free mode and the card reader is turned off, so guests use it as often as they like without paying. On other days it runs as normal, with guests paying per session.

Do we own the booth?

Yes. Retro Booth sells photo booths for you to own, either outright or with finance, subject to eligibility.

Can we brand the booth for our venue?

Yes. Venue branding can be applied to the cabinet and signage, subject to availability.

Do our staff need to run it?

No. Guests use the booth themselves, so your team can stay focused on service.

Are there ongoing costs?

Yes. Every booth runs with an ongoing software and support subscription. We'll explain exactly what's included before you commit.

What about guest data?

Data capture is optional. If you use it for marketing, guests must give their consent.
