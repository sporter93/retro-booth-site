Ownership & finance

Apply for photo booth finance

Spread the cost of your booth instead of paying upfront. Finance is arranged through our finance partner, TAFS. Complete the application below and they'll assess it and be in touch. Finance is subject to approval.

  • Before you start: have your business details (ABN and trading name) and ID handy. The form may ask you to use your device's camera to verify your ID.
  • Not sure which booth yet? Talk to us first and we'll help you choose a model and prepare a quote.

Finance application form

The application form is provided by TAFS. Information you enter goes directly to TAFS and is handled under their privacy policy. Retro Booth doesn't see or store it. Approval, rates and terms are set by TAFS. Talk to your accountant about tax.
