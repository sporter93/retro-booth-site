Own or share?

Photo booth revenue share vs buying: which is better for your venue?

It depends on how busy the booth will be. On revenue share, an operator supplies the booth for free and keeps an agreed share of every session. When you buy, you keep every session's takings but cover the booth, consumables and the software subscription. Busy booths usually earn more owned. Quiet ones can be better shared.

The honest answer

When revenue share is the better choice

Owning isn't right for every venue. Revenue share can make more sense when:

  • The booth will only see a handful of sessions a week, so your monthly costs would outweigh the share you'd keep
  • You want to see how guests use a booth before committing to buy one
  • The space is temporary, seasonal or likely to change
  • You'd rather not have the booth as an asset on your books

If your booth is busy most nights, the share you give away each month usually adds up to more than the cost of owning one.

FAQ

Questions about revenue share and buying

Is a revenue share photo booth worth it?

It can be, especially for quieter venues or as a trial, because there's usually no upfront cost. The trade-off is that the operator keeps part of every session for as long as the booth is there. For a busy booth, that share can cost more than owning one outright.

Who owns the booth on a revenue share deal?

The operator does. They supply the booth and take it back when the agreement ends. When you buy from Retro Booth, the booth is yours.

Can I buy a photo booth on finance?

Yes. Finance is available as an alternative to paying upfront, subject to eligibility. Repayments depend on the booth, the term and approval, so we include them in your quote.

What ongoing costs come with owning a booth?

Every Retro Booth runs on a software and support subscription. You also cover paper and ink, and the booth's power. We set out the subscription cost in your quote.

How many sessions a week do I need to break even?

Divide your monthly ownership cost by the money you'd give away per session on revenue share, then by 4.33 weeks. Use the calculator on this page with your own figures to see the answer for your venue.

Can I switch from revenue share to owning a booth?

Usually, yes. Check when your current agreement ends and any notice period it requires, then talk to us about having your own booth ready to go in.
